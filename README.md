I created this project to save me time when compilling a list of games/events and contact information. The goal was to quickly produce a list of collegiate sporting games that I could soliciate my photography services for. I typically target visiting teams as most schools typically have someone on staff to handle home games. The distance calculators are there to prefilter the composite calender, since those are the only game/event sites I am concerned with and provide flexibility in the case I move outside of Charlotte. 

The composited staff directory tries to filter for communications and creative staff as these are the people that typically hire and deal with freelancers. This final filtered staff directory then feeds into my Google Sheets calender/track as a filtered dropdown for the staff members name. Once the name is inputed the email and phone number of the staff member get autofilled. The dropdown uses the game information to dyanamically produce each row's name dropdown.

I recall trying to do this manually and it took the full day to create for all of UNC Chapel Hills spring 2026 games. Around 100 pre-season and regualar season game from my tracker. This script takes about an hour to compile the full staff directory and another 10 or so minutes for the game schedules. This gave me about 350 or so pre-season and regular season games up to the beginning of November across 11 Charlotte area colleges/univesities.
